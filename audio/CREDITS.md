# Audio Credits

The sound effects in this game are recordings and designs by the people named below, each released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each file in `audio/sfx/` has been made mono and cut to length, with a short fade and a level change, and is otherwise the maker's sound. Credit is given here although CC0 does not ask for it.

There is no music yet.

- **Casino Audio**, by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of playing a card. https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- **RPG Audio**, by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of drawing a card, the sound of an enemy being hit, the coins counted after a fight and the coins spent in the shop. https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **Impact Sounds**, by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of Block, the bell when Passion crosses into a new tier and the blow when you are hurt. https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **UI Audio**, by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. It gives the button click. https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- **Fantasy Sound Effects (Tinysized SFX)**, by Vehicle (Jan Schupke, vehiclemusic.eu), CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of a status effect being applied, the hammer blow of a card upgrade and the sound of an enemy falling. https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-sound-effects-tinysized-sfx
- **Fire Staff Sound Effects**, by LEGIT Audio, CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of a card being exhausted. https://opengameart.org/content/fire-staff-sound-effects
- **Trumpet Fanfare**, by Graciano Choc (gchoc, Bz.gamedev), CC0 1.0. It gives the fanfare for a won fight. https://opengameart.org/content/trumpet-fanfare
- **Lose Game Short Music Clip**, by Robin Lamb, CC0 1.0. It gives the sound of a lost fight. https://opengameart.org/content/lose-game-short-music-clip
- **Magic Sound Effects**, by Polar_34, CC0 1.0. It gives the chime when you gain a relic. https://polar-34.itch.io/magic-sound-effects
- **Magic Words + Healing Sound Effect**, by Julie Damsgaard (Spring Spring, spring-enterprises.neocities.org), CC0 1.0. It gives the healing sound at a campfire. https://opengameart.org/content/magic-words-healing-sound-effect
